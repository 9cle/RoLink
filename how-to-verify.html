How to Verify

Follow these quick steps to link your Roblox account with Discord.

1

Run the Verify Command

Go to your Discord server and type /verify in any channel where RoLink has permission to respond.

2

Authorize via Roblox

Click the Verify with Roblox button in Discord. Log in on the official Roblox site to grant read-only permissions.

3

Copy Your Verification Command

You will be redirected back here to receive your command code (e.g., /complete code:...).

4

Complete in Discord

Paste and execute the /complete command in Discord to sync your server roles and nickname automatically.
