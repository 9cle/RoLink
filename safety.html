How We Handle Safety

Your account security and privacy are our highest priorities.

🔒 Secure OAuth 2.0 Authentication

RoLink uses Roblox's official OAuth 2.0 system. We never ask for your Roblox password, cookie (.ROBLOSECURITY), or sensitive account credentials. You log in directly on Roblox.com.

🛡️ Read-Only Account Access

The permissions requested during authentication are strictly limited to basic user details (User ID, Username, and Group Ranks). RoLink cannot modify your Roblox account, spend Robux, or access private messages.

💾 Minimal Data Retention

We only store your Discord User ID linked to your Roblox User ID and Username for server role sync. We do not sell, share, or track user data outside of functional bot verification features.

⚡ One-Time Authorization Codes

Authorization codes generated during login expire quickly and can only be redeemed once via Discord slash commands, preventing interception or unauthorized re-use.
